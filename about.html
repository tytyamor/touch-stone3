About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Founded in 2026, North Star Bakery was born out of a deep love for traditional Parisian

baking combined with the cozy, welcoming charm of a hometown café. What started as a

small neighborhood dream has blossomed into a daily gathering place for fresh bread,

warm smiles, and community.
